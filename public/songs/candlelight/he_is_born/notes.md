## Doubts
1. m. 48 "-joice/-vè-": I read Eb4 (two ledger lines above). It is measured at 2 interlines above the top line; the Db4 notes in m. 47 sit clearly in the space above one ledger line. It could be Db4 if the photo misleads me, but Eb4 (V) going to Eb3 makes musical sense.
2. m. 40 "now": the half-note chord read as Eb3+G3 (the noteheads are hollow and partly merged with the staff lines). It could be a unison Eb3 like m. 36, but there are two noteheads.
3. m. 36 / 40 beats 1-2: the two noteheads touch and are offset sideways. I read Db3+F3 (rows give a span from the F3 line to the D3 line). The alternative is Eb3+F3 (a second).
4. m. 45: descending eighths Ab3 G3 F3 Eb3 under "Now_ is_" (slurs). The first note is on the top line (Ab3), not Bb3.
5. m. 46 "Christ Child": a single C3 (there is no divisi).
6. Repeat structure and starting point: see above. The circled sign at m. 33 and the "(circled) 33" at the foot of p.4 are hard to read.
7. Mm. 1-8 intro omitted. If the choir performs them with a long rest, add 8 measures of r/1 at the start.
