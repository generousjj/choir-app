# Candlelight Carol (John Rutter, SATBB and organ, OUP) - Bass transcription notes

## Pages used
- A-012 cover (it says "SATBB and organ", so the bass divides into Bass 1 and Bass 2 in places).
- A-013 (p.3, mm 1-20), A-014 (p.4, mm 21-30), A-015 (p.5, mm 31-40, 2nd ending 72, 73-77; photographed at an angle and curved),
  A-016 (p.6, mm 78-97), A-017 (p.7, mm 98-107, ends with a final double bar and fermatas at m107).
- No bar is physically missing from the photos. All 107 bars were read, so no rests were added to fill gaps.

## Measure range and structure (performance order, 107 measures)
- 1-4: organ intro (rests).
- 5-20: Verse 1 is "SOPRANOS (and ALTOS)" only, so the men have rests.
- 21-35: refrain, SATBB (verse 1 is unaccompanied). 36 is the 1st ending ("born."), and 37-40 are the organ interlude (rests).
- Repeat ("BACK TO START" in pencil, written as the repeat bar at m5). I used the score's own second-time numbers:
  41-56 is Verse 2 (TENORS and BASSES), 57-71 is the refrain again, and 72 is the 2nd ending ("born.").
- 73-74: rests. 75-86 is Verse 3 (T and B on one staff; I took the stems-down notes). 87-90: A.T.B. "Hum".
- 91-98: men "unis." on the melody (Candlelight, angel light ... breaking of dawn). 99-102: Gloria / in excelsis Deo.
- 103-107: "Hum" to the end (rall., fermata on the last chord).

## Which line
- Verse 2 (41-56): the single voice staff in treble-8 clef, which tenors and basses sing in unison. I wrote it at sounding pitch (down to Cb3, up to F4).
- Refrain and Gloria (21-35, 57-71, 99-102): the **Bass 1** staff. The singer crossed out the whole Bass 2 staff in pencil (scribbles on pp.4, 5 and 7), so B2 is not written.
- Where the bass divides on one stem, I wrote a chord: m36, m72 and m90 are `Gb2+Db3`.
- mm 88-89: the lower divisi notes are crossed out in pencil, so only the upper note is written.

## Tempo
- Printed: "Andante con moto, quarter = 92", so `tempo: 92`. There is a rall. near the end; I put it as text at m106 (its exact bar is uncertain).

## Pencil marks followed
- "BACK TO START" at the 1st ending, which is the repeat.
- B2 staff crossed out, which I treated as "sing Bass 1".
- Verse-1 lyrics are greyed or struck on p.3, which fits men tacet in verse 1.
- Circled breath marks: m22/58 after "light", m24/60 after "glow", m28/64 after "dawn", m32/68 after "o!". These are not encoded.
- "B1/2" and "careful" at m75 mean both basses sing this. Arrows at m84 mark the octave leap Bb2 to Bb3.
- "Hum oooh" circled at m87 and "Hum ooooo" at m103 are written as the lyric "Hum".

## Doubts
1. m36 (1st ending): the chord is covered by pencil circles. I wrote Gb2+Db3, the same as the clearly printed m72. The lower note could be Ab2.
2. mm 88-90 (Hum): I read m88 as Eb3 half and Gb3 quarter tied into m89 Gb3 half, then F3, then m90 Gb2+Db3. The lower notes in 88-89 are crossed out, and the photo is curved here.
3. m87: Fb3 (an explicit flat on F, dotted half). It is clear, but it is unusual.
4. m29/65/99: I read "Glo-" as Db4 and the next two quarters as Eb4. The 2nd ledger line is hidden in the noteheads, so they could be Db4.
5. m81: the dotted Ab3 has a pencil circle around the dot. I kept the printed rhythm (dotted quarter, eighth, quarter).
6. m97: "of" has a printed natural, so Cn4.
7. Verse 2 m43 "kneel" is Cb3, a 7th drop from Bb3. I checked this on the photo.
8. Lyric underlay at mm 87-90: ATB hum while the sopranos sing "A child with his mother...". I wrote "Hum" once at m87; the notes after it carry no new syllable.
9. Verse 2 is a unison tenor+bass melody, and the high notes (Eb4/F4 at m50) are as printed. Only the singer can say whether the basses take it down an octave.

## Lyric language
English, with Latin "Gloria" and "in excelsis Deo".
