- m.105-120: the men's staff is tenor (stems up) + bass (stems down). The singer crossed out (X) the
  upper notes in m.106-108, 111-112 and 114-120, so only the LOWER (bass) line is written there.
  m.105 has no X, but the two voices have different rhythms there, so I also wrote the bass only.
  The tenor line there is Eb4 D4 C4.
- m.121-128: up to three men's parts. Chords are written as bass + middle voice, and the tenor top note is in the comments.
  In m.127-128 the singer crossed out the LOW Eb3, so he sings the Bb3 (the upper chord note).
