# Silent Night (Healey, Candlelight 2004): S/A/T parts notes

## Staff layout (pp. 1-5, mm. 1-70)
- Choir staves: "Soprano/Alto" (one treble staff) and "Tenor/Bass" (one bass-clef staff). Single noteheads
  throughout mm. 35-70: no divisi on either staff.
- So S.txt = A.txt (same notes, unison S/A line), and T.txt = the bass file's line (T/B unison), note for note.

## What each part sings
- mm. 1-34: choir tacet (rests in all parts). Solo Tenor sings Spanish verse 1 in mm. 1-29 (left out);
  mm. 30-34 piano interlude.
- mm. 35-58 (A major, mp): verse 1 in English. S/A sing the melody; T/B sing the same melody an octave lower
  (checked note by note: S/A = bass +1 octave, same rhythm and lyrics).
- mm. 59-60: choir rests.
- mm. 61-70 (B-flat major, mf): verse 2 in English. S/A sing the melody (F G F | D | F G F | D | C C | A |
  Bb Bb | F | G G | Bb. A G); T/B sing a harmony line under it. The Solo Tenor sings Spanish verse 2 above
  (mm. 62-70, "Noche de paz, noche de amor, Todo duerme en derredor. Desde los"): left out.
- Lyrics are the same in all parts (shared English text). No "Hum"/"Christ child" adaptations apply here.

## Ranges / doubts
1. S/A go down to A3 in mm. 57-58 ("peace.") as printed (unison melody); low for sopranos, but printed so.
2. Tenor = the T/B line, which goes down to A2 (mm. 57-58) and E3/C#3 in verse 1: below usual tenor range,
   but it is a single unison T/B line on the page.
3. Bass-file doubts 1-3 (mm. 65, 67, 69-70) carry over to T.txt since it is the same line. The harmony with
   the women supports the bass file's readings. No errors found in the bass file.
4. Score ends at m. 70 (page 6 onward missing), mid-verse 2.
