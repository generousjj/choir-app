5. m.19 soprano: two sharps by the chord. Only F#5 needs one (the notes are F#5+A5). I did not find a second sharpened note.
6. Ties on chords (m.19 to m.20 in S and T) may not show as ties in the MusicXML (the same converter limit as in the bass notes).
